# Studio workspace

Scope: the Nymphaea Studio workspace and landing (src/index.html #maker, src/maker/*). Mode: Operate.

Audience and job: writers, readers and small presses turn a text into an illustrated book, place and tune pictures, choose a look, export. The live book is the centre. The Markdown editor is closed by default and opens on request.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The book is a live galley proof on the table and every Studio control is a quiet mark around it in forest green that never touches the words. It refuses the three equal panes of a generic editor with a lavender accent.

OWN-WORLD: User re-theme, binding: jet black room (60%), space gray floating panels (30%), forest green (10%) only for marks, the active state and the one primary action (Export); pollen amber for warnings. Dark by default, light mode in cool grey. Archivo (condensed widths) for the interface, Geist Mono only for counts and paragraph numbers, the book's own face for quoted captions. Panels float with 12 to 14px radii and soft shadows; inside them, sections are ruled, not boxed.

APPROVAL: The user approved the static mockup .impeccable/mocks/studio.html on 2026-11-11 ("nice but change this bluish theme ... 60 jet black, 30 space gray and 10 forest green"), then asked for more power and more book settings. The re-themed mockup is the critique reference.

STORY: The writer sees their book at once, sees where the pictures are as green marks on a thumb index, opens a picture or a view, changes the look live, exports.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with the lotus, title, a mono slug of words and pictures with the save state, the view switch (Book, Storyboard, Story line, Compare) in the centre, the ⌘K command bar, undo, colours, menu, and Export as the one green action at top right. A 64px rail (Book, Text, Models, hide panel). A 320px panel with Chapters and Pictures, the Markdown closed. The live book in the centre with a thumb index on its fore edge and a dock for device, layout and zoom. A 300px Design inspector on the right.

FORM: The Galley Proof, position 4 on my ordered list, seed 8fd9588b. Raises: thumb index (Thumb Index Manual), Story line view (Story Line), focus grows in Storyboard (Title Card Wall), struck marks that restore (Ticket Wallet), live controls with no apply step (Type Specimen), rationed accent (Monochrome Product).

Signature interaction: the thumb index. Hovering a caret shows its quoted line, clicking scrolls the galley there; the tab of the chapter in view extends. Motion is 160ms ease-out, state only.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
