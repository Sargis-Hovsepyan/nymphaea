IThe lamp

The lamp above the table buzzed quietly, its yellow glow pooling over the chessboard like a spotlight meant for a confession. The rest of the room was darker than it needed to be. My father always preferred half-shadows.

“They make people show their real faces,” he used to say. It was ironic; he wore his own face like a wall.

We sat across from each other like two architects forced to collaborate on a structure neither wanted to share.
