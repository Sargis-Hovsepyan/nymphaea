      Nymphaea

      Your books

        Illustrated books from plain text

        Every passage, drawn from its own words.

        Drop in a manuscript. Nymphaea reads each passage for its place, its hour, the weather, the things in it and what moves, then draws a picture that acts it out. Read the book on a screen, in spreads, on paper or on an e-reader. Your words are never changed.

        PDF, Word, EPUB, web pages, Markdown or text. Drop files anywhere on this page, or a whole series at once. Files stay on this device.

        How a sentence becomes a picture

        No model is needed. Every picture is composed by rules you can read, so the same words always give the same drawing. A model on your own computer can paint over it if you want.

        1. 01It readsWord lists find the place, the hour, the weather, the season, the things, who is there and what they do. A place named after “in” or “at” counts double. Anything the text says is absent does not count.
        2. 02It carriesA chapter’s setting carries forward and fades, so “She walked on” still happens in the street at night the chapter set up. A place keeps its look, and a named person keeps their silhouette.
        3. 03It actsVerbs become motion. Smoke leaves the chimney that is in the picture, sparks rise from the flame, leaves fall in autumn, a flock crosses away from the light, and a star falls only in a night sky.

        What goes in and what comes out

        In

        • PDFPage numbers and running heads come out, split paragraphs are joined, italics are kept.
        • Word, EPUBHeadings, italics and line breaks become Markdown.
        • Web pagesOr paste straight from Word or Google Docs.
        • Markdown, textOne file, or a whole series ordered by file name.

        Out

        • HTML bookOne file. It works offline and can go straight onto GitHub Pages.
        • EPUBFor Apple Books, Kobo and other e-readers.
        • PrintTrim sizes, mirrored margins, page numbers. Save it as a PDF.
        • MarkdownThe text itself, to keep or take elsewhere.

        Your words stay yours.

        Nymphaea only adds things around the text: pictures, spacing, type. A caption that isn’t an exact quote of your paragraph is replaced before it reaches the page, and every import is tested to keep every paragraph.

              Formatting help
              
        Start a line with # for a chapter or ## for a section. > makes a quote, and under a heading an epigraph. End a line with \ to keep its line break, for verse. *word* is italic, **word** bold, and * * * a scene break. Pasting from Word or Google Docs keeps italics and headings.
